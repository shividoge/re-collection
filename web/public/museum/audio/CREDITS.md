# Museum music

`gymnopedie-1.mp3`: Erik Satie, *Gymnopédie No. 1* ("Lent et douloureux"), performed by Robin
Alciatore for Musopen. Public domain (composition and recording). Source: Wikimedia Commons,
`File:Erik_Satie_-_gymnopedies_-_la_1_ere._lent_et_douloureux.ogg`, MP3 transcode.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Erik_Satie_-_gymnopedies_-_la_1_ere._lent_et_douloureux.ogg

The step-in and step-out sounds are synthesized in the browser (web/src/museum/sound.ts); there
are no sound files for them.
